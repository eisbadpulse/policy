Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the Eisbad Pulse mobile application (collectively, the "Application") provided by MaKro ("Service Provider").

1. Data Collection and Health Data

The Application is designed as a local tracking utility for cold exposure and heart rate monitoring. All session data, ice bath timers, and heart rate metrics processed via Android Health Services / Wear OS sensors are stored strictly locally on your device.

  • No Account Required: You can use the Application without registering, logging in, or providing personal identifiers (such as name or email address).
  • Health & Sensor Data: Heart rate metrics and timer durations are collected solely to display real-time feedback and local history within the app. This data is not transmitted to external servers operated by the Service Provider.

2. Data Deletion Request (Google Play Policy Compliance)

How to delete your data:

Because all session logs, preferences, and sensor metrics are stored exclusively on your local device storage:

  • App Uninstall: Simply uninstalling the Application from your Android / Wear OS device will permanently and immediately delete all stored data, session history, and cached information from your device.
  • Manual In-App Clear: You can also clear local app data at any time via your Android System Settings (Settings > Apps > Eisbad Pulse > Storage > Clear Data).

Since no user data is stored on remote servers or cloud databases, no further request is required to remove your information from external systems.

3. Third-Party Services & Financial Transactions

The Application utilizes standard third-party frameworks for core store functionality and optional tipping:

  • Google Play Services (for app distribution and optional in-app tipping via Google Play Billing).

No personal data, tracking cookies, or health metrics are shared with advertising networks or data brokers.

4. Security

Because your health metrics and session logs remain in your device's isolated local application storage, your data benefits directly from Android's built-in sandbox security architecture.

5. Children's Privacy

The Application is intended for adult users (18 years and older) interested in cold exposure and fitness tracking. The Service Provider does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
